Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple and by RevenueCat, our subscription service. To restore a purchase, use Restore a purchase in Settings > Subscription in the app. Deleting your StartBy account does not cancel your App Store subscription; cancel it in the App Store.

Deleting your account

Delete account, in Settings, removes your StartBy account and StartBy data from our server and clears your personal StartBy data from your iPhone. Display preferences may remain on the device.
