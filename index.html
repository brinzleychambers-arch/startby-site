Privacy Policy

What StartBy collects, what leaves your phone, and what never does.

Last updated 5 August 2026

The short version. Almost everything StartBy knows about you stays on your phone. Your events, your trips, your settings and everything the app has learned about how long places take you are stored on the device and are not uploaded anywhere. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your phone

All of the following is stored on your device only. It is not sent to us, and it is not sent to anyone else:

  • The events you add — their names, addresses and times
  • Your trip history, including when you left and when you arrived
  • Everything the app has learned: how long each place takes you to get into, how long you take to get ready, how long after being told to leave you usually leave
  • Your settings, including your chosen colour and your timings
  • The name and phone number of anyone you have chosen to notify if you are running late

Deleting the app removes all of it.

What leaves your phone, and why

Addresses, to work out travel time

To tell you when to leave, StartBy has to ask how long the journey takes. When it does, it sends where you are starting from and where you are going to our server, which passes them to Google Maps Platform and returns the travel time. Google's handling of that request is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

The same happens when you search for an address: what you have typed, and roughly where you are, are sent so the suggestions are nearby ones. Nothing about those searches is stored on our server.

Crash reports, so faults can be found

If StartBy fails, a report is sent to Sentry, a crash reporting service, so the fault can be found and fixed. The report contains the error and where in the code it happened, along with your device model and iOS version.

It does not contain your events, your addresses, your location, or anything you have typed. StartBy is configured not to send personal information, and the details of network requests are stripped from these reports before they leave the phone. Sentry's handling is covered by Sentry's Privacy Policy.

An identifier, to run the free trial

If you sign in with Apple, StartBy stores the identifier Apple gives it, along with the date your free trial started. That is the whole record. It exists so the trial cannot be restarted by deleting and reinstalling the app. If Apple gives us your name, it is used only to address you in the app.

We also count how many route requests each identifier makes, so that one person cannot run up a bill for everyone. That count is a number, not a history of where you went.

Location

StartBy uses your location for two things, and both are visible to you.

Working out when to leave. The app checks where you are when you open it or add an event, so it can measure the journey from where you actually are.

Noticing when you leave and arrive, if you turn it on. This is optional and off unless you allow it. When it is on, your phone watches for you crossing into or out of two areas: roughly where you are setting off from, and where you are going. Crossing them is what lets the app check its own estimates against what really happened.

  • StartBy does not record a trail of where you have been
  • Your coordinates are not sent to our server
  • The two areas are only watched around the time you are due to leave
  • You can turn automatic logging off at any time in the app, under Settings, and everything still works — you just tap when you leave and when you arrive instead

Your calendar

If you allow it, StartBy reads your calendar so it can offer the events you have to travel to. It reads the title, the location and the time, and only for events in the next week. It never writes to your calendar except when you edit an event you created yourself, and it will not alter anything with other people invited.

Your calendar is read on your phone. Its contents are not uploaded.

Your contacts

StartBy never asks for your address book. When you choose someone to notify, your phone shows you its own contact picker and hands back only the one person you picked. That name and number are stored on your device so the app can offer them again. Messages are written for you but never sent — you press send yourself, in your own Messages app.

What StartBy does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, and no third-party trackers
  • Nothing is sold or shared with data brokers
  • No profile is built about you

Deleting your data

Everything can be deleted from inside the app, in one step, under Settings → Starting over → Delete your account. That removes the trial record held against your Apple identifier from our server, and every piece of data StartBy holds on your phone. It cannot be undone and it does not need to go through us.

If you only want to clear what is on the phone and keep your trial record, Settings → Starting over → Reset everything does that instead. Deleting the app also removes everything held on the phone.

Signing in with Apple is revoked by you, not by the app: iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple. No app can do that on your behalf.

Children

StartBy is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Anything significant will be said in the app.

Support
