StartBy Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 August 2026

StartBy works out when you need to start getting ready so that you arrive on time. To do that it needs to know where you are going and roughly where you are setting off from. This page explains exactly what it collects, what leaves your phone, and what never does.

StartBy does not sell your data. There is no advertising in the app, and no advertiser, data broker or analytics company receives anything about you.

What stays on your phone

Almost everything. The following is stored only on your device and is never uploaded:

  • Your events — their names, addresses, times and reminders
  • Your trip history — every departure and arrival StartBy has recorded, and the times it measured
  • What StartBy has learned about you: how long your journeys usually take, how long it takes you to park and walk in, how long you take to get out of the door
  • Your settings, and the name of anyone you have chosen to text

If you delete the app, all of this goes with it.

Location

StartBy asks for location access so it can time your journeys. What it does with that is limited and specific.

Working out a route. When StartBy needs a travel time, the place you are setting off from and the place you are going are sent to StartBy's own server. That server asks Google for a route and returns only the number of minutes. Google is never told who you are, and StartBy's server does not keep a record of these one-off requests.

Rechecking traffic. If your journey is one StartBy is watching, those two points are stored on StartBy's server together with the times of the event, so the traffic can be checked again while your phone is closed. Nothing else about the event is stored — not its name, not who is going, not your calendar.

Those points are deleted automatically within a day of the event happening. They are kept only for as long as there is a journey left to check.

Knowing you arrived. If you allow location access in the background, your phone alone works out when you have left and when you have arrived. That happens on the device. The result is a time, stored on your phone, and it is not uploaded.

StartBy does not track you continuously and does not keep a record of where you have been.

Your calendar

If you allow it, StartBy reads the titles, times and places of your upcoming events so you can choose which ones to be on time for. It reads nothing else — not the notes, not the guest list.

StartBy can change one thing in your calendar: if you correct an event's time inside StartBy, it updates that event so the two do not disagree. It only ever does this for an event that has no other guests, because an event with guests is somebody else's meeting.

Your calendar is not uploaded. The address of an event you choose is used the same way any other address is — to work out a route.

Contacts

If you ask StartBy to text somebody when you are running late, it opens your phone's own contact picker and receives only the one person you choose. StartBy never reads your address book.

Nothing is ever sent on your behalf. StartBy writes the message and opens it for you; you press send.

Signing in

StartBy uses Sign in with Apple. Apple gives the app an identifier for you, and your name and email address if you choose to share them. The identifier is stored on StartBy's server so that your free trial follows you to a new phone. Your name and email stay on your device.

You can stop sharing your email at any time in iPhone Settings, under your Apple Account.

Crash reports

If StartBy stops unexpectedly, a report is sent to Sentry, a crash-reporting service, so the fault can be found and fixed. These reports contain technical information only: the error, and where in the app it happened.

They do not contain your addresses, your event names, your calendar, or your location. Web addresses are removed from the report before it is sent, in case one of them carries a place name.

Payments

If you subscribe, the payment is handled entirely by Apple. StartBy never sees your card details and never receives them.

Children

StartBy is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Deleting everything

You can delete your account from inside the app, in Settings. This removes what is held on StartBy's server and everything on your phone.

Sign in with Apple itself is revoked by you, in iPhone Settings under your Apple Account. No app is allowed to do that for you.

You can also write to the address below and ask for anything held about you to be deleted.

Who processes what

  • Supabase — hosts StartBy's server, which holds your Apple identifier, your trial start date, and the start and end points of journeys being watched
  • Google Maps Platform — receives coordinates and addresses in order to return routes and address suggestions, and is never told who they belong to
  • Sentry — receives crash reports, as described above
  • Apple — handles sign-in and any payment
